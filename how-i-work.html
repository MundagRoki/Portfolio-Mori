How I Work

Ажлын урсгал  ·  brief to production

Ажлын урсгал

Twelve steps across five stages, with three points where somebody else gets a say. The gates are the whole point — the fastest way to lose a month is to disappear with a spec and come back with a finished design nobody asked for.

01Brief & spec received

The work starts when somebody writes down what this is for, who it is for, and what counts as done. A spec you can argue with beats a spec you have to guess at.

Most wasted design work solves a problem nobody had. The wireframes at the end of this stage are deliberately rough — they exist to test whether the thinking holds, not to look finished.

Алхам  ·  Steps

  1. 01 Brief & spec received

    The work starts when somebody writes down what this is for, who it is for, and what counts as done. A spec you can argue with beats a spec you have to guess at.

  2. 02 Brainstorm

    Everything plausible goes on the board before anything gets judged. Narrowing too early is how you end up building the first idea instead of the best one.

  3. 03 Deep research AI-assisted, using my own master prompt

    Competitors, conventions and the people who will actually use it, run through a master prompt I keep tuning. It turns a week of reading into an afternoon.

  4. 04 Userflows and wireframes Built with Claude

    Every screen and every branch, drawn rough on purpose. If a flow only reads when the visuals are finished, the flow is doing none of the work.

Review with the PO

05Mid-fidelity screens

Real layout, real hierarchy, no decoration. Grey boxes keep the argument on the right things — where attention goes, and what is missing.

Layout, hierarchy and flow are settled here because this is the last point where changing them is cheap. Structural decisions deferred past this stage cost far more to undo.

Алхам  ·  Steps

  1. 05 Mid-fidelity screens

    Real layout, real hierarchy, no decoration. Grey boxes keep the argument on the right things — where attention goes, and what is missing.

  2. 06 Cover every edge case

    Empty, loading, error, offline, and the string that runs too long. Settling them here is cheap; settling them after the visuals are signed off is not.

Review with the PO

07High-fidelity screens

Type, colour, spacing and every state, resolved until nothing is left to interpret. What stays ambiguous here becomes somebody else’s guess later.

Type, colour, spacing and interaction, resolved until there is nothing left to interpret. Anything still ambiguous at this point becomes somebody else’s decision later.

  1. 07 High-fidelity screens

    Type, colour, spacing and every state, resolved until nothing is left to interpret. What stays ambiguous here becomes somebody else’s guess later.

Review with the PO and stakeholders

08Build the design system

Components, variants and tokens, named so they survive the next three features. The system is what makes the second screen cheap to build.

Finished screens are not a deliverable — a system is. Components, tokens and motion described precisely enough that the build matches the file the first time.

Алхам  ·  Steps

  1. 08 Build the design system

    Components, variants and tokens, named so they survive the next three features. The system is what makes the second screen cheap to build.

  2. 09 Haptics and animation spec A spreadsheet engineering builds from

    Trigger, duration, easing and feel, written out per interaction so engineering can build straight from it. Motion described in adjectives ships as nothing.

10QA — Figma against production

Screen against screen, at the same size, in the same light. Most of what drifts between file and build is a few pixels nobody wrote down.

The stage most designers skip. Figma checked against production, a fix list written, and a final audit before anything ships.

Алхам  ·  Steps

  1. 10 QA — Figma against production

    Screen against screen, at the same size, in the same light. Most of what drifts between file and build is a few pixels nobody wrote down.

  2. 11 Fix list for engineering

    Every gap logged with a screenshot, a location and a severity. A fix list is a queue somebody can work through; a vague complaint is an argument.

  3. 12 Final audit before release

    One pass across the whole flow before it ships, in the order a real person would meet it. The last look is what catches whatever screen-by-screen missed.
